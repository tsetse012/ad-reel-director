# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-09-07

First public release. Extracted from three productions (two Jupnyangi spots, one Nunchi spot).

- `SKILL.md` — the seven steps, two render routes, prompt rules, QA gate
- `templates/` — concept style, sheet rules, keyframe identity block, 30 s prompt skeleton, moveboard
- `scripts/` — `gen_stills.py` (concepts / sheets / keyframes from a JSON job file), `crop_panels.py`,
  `render_seedance.py` (i2v and r2v, resumable), `qa_frames.py`, `assemble_endcard.sh`, `mix_bgm.sh`
- `examples/` — moveboards, exact prompts, keyframe and QA strips, captions for all three spots, plus the i2v vs r2v A/B
